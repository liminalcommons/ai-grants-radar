Sources: 4 parallel scouts, every item verified on its live official page. These are NOT radar grants (radar has zero São Paulo entries) — do not mix into grants.json matches.

## TRACK A — no CNPJ needed, actionable now

1. PNAB Taboão da Serra 08/2026 (fomento) — R$1.26M total, 82 projects. Coletivo sem CNPJ applies via pessoa física. Deadline Nov 9, 23:59 BRT. GATE: requires residing + acting in Taboão da Serra 2+ years + artist cadastro. If Coexistir is capital-based, blocked.
   https://culteditais.cultura.gov.br/files/opportunity/11803/edital-de-chamamento-publico-no-08-2026.pdf
2. Taboão 07/2026 Prêmio Pontos de Cultura — R$120k, 10 prêmios, no prestação de contas. Same Nov 9 deadline, same Taboão 2-year gate.
   https://culturaviva.cultura.gov.br/files/opportunity/12100/chamamento-publico-de-premiacao-para-pontos-e-pontoes-de-cultura-07-2026.pdf
3. PIAPI credenciamento 38/2025 (SMC-SP) — permanently open, pessoa física, R$65/h (~R$3.4–4.7k/mo, ~10 months). Articulador de Território or Eixo 4 (oralidades) fits the rodas. NOT a grant: service contract, no call guarantee, presencial in municipal equipment, Wednesdays 10h–13h mandatory.
   https://portadeentrada.prefeitura.sp.gov.br/piapi/
4. Funarte Aberta 2026 (Complexo Funarte SP) — free venue incl. convivência space, pessoa física allowed, continuous flow to Apr 30 2027. No cash, but a free home for gatherings.
   https://www.gov.br/funarte/pt-br/editais/2026/programa-funarte-aberta/programa-funarte-aberta-2026-2013-ocupacao-dos-espacos-culturais-da-funarte-complexo-funarte-sao-paulo
5. Pollination Project Daily Grants — up to US$500, rolling (month-end cutoff, decision 6–12 wks). Individuals + informal groups, Brazil ok. Frame meals plant-based or bill non-food costs (venue, transport); no pay for own time; needs wire-transfer account.
   https://thepollinationproject.org/apply/
6. Peace First — youth 16–35 only, individuals/informal groups, Brazil ok, peacebuilding fit + mentorship. Confirm open round on their site/Instagram.
   https://peacefirst.org/grants/
7. Cozinha Solidária habilitação (MDS) — continuous, no explicit CNPJ, kitchen running 6+ months required. Gateway to maintenance funds + PAA food. 30-day decision, 4-yr validity.
   https://www.gov.br/pt-br/servicos/solicitar-habilitacao-da-cozinha-solidaria
8. Ponto de Cultura certification — continuous, free, coletivo sem CNPJ ok, needs 2+ yrs proven activity. Unlocks premiações + bonus in PNAB/Cultura Viva calls.
   https://www.gov.br/culturaviva/pt-br/cadastro-nacional-cultura-viva
9. Lei Rouanet pessoa física via Salic — by Oct 31. NOT cash: only authorizes sponsor fundraising. Formalization path only.
   https://www.gov.br/cultura/pt-br/assuntos/lei-rouanet
