Acme AI Safety Grants
Applications are open to registered US nonprofits and companies only.
Applicants must be a legal entity incorporated in the United States.
Grants of up to $150,000 are awarded for 12 months.
Applications open March 1, 2026. Submissions close July 15, 2026.
Decisions will be announced September 1, 2026.
You must submit a two-page project proposal and a budget.
